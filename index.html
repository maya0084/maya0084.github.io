How to play
Use keyboard on desktop or the on-screen buttons and gestures on touch devices. Tap to rotate. Swipe left/right to move. Swipe down to soft-drop. Tap ⤓ to hard-drop.
Tips
  • Clear multiple lines for higher score.
  • Rotate near walls with kicks (implemented).
  • Use hold-down on the drop button for fast soft-drop.
